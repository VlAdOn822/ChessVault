# Repertoire (simple, for 600-900)

Principle: less theory, more understanding. Control the center, develop pieces, castle.

## As White
- **London System** (1.d4, Nf3, Bf4, e3, c3, Bd3): nearly the same setup against almost everything.
- Add notes per line as you lose games in them.

## As Black
- **Against 1.e4:** 1...e5, then develop knights and bishops, castle.
- **Against 1.d4:** 1...d5, then Nf6, e6, Bd6 (or c5).
- **Against anything else (1.Nf3, 1.c4):** 1...d5 and the same setup.

## Traps to know (defensively)
- Scholar's Mate (Qh5 and Bc4 against f7)
- Fried Liver Attack in the Italian Game
