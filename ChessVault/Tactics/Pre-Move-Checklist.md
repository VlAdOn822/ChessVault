# Pre-Move Checklist

Ask these five questions before every move:

1. **What does my opponent want?** What threat does their last move create?
2. **What is under attack on my side?** Are all my pieces protected?
3. **What can I capture or attack?** Checks, captures, threats, in that order.
4. **Where am I going?** After my move, is anything hanging? Is there a check or fork in reply?
5. Only then, play the move.

> If you catch yourself thinking "it will probably be fine", you skipped question 1 or 4.
