# Training Plan (beginner, roughly 600 to 900 rating)

Goal for the first 2-3 months: **stop hanging pieces.** At this level games are decided by who blunders first, not by opening theory.

## Daily (20-30 minutes)
- [ ] 15-20 puzzles on Chess.com (tactics, take your time)
- [ ] 1-2 games at 10+0 or 15+10 (skip 3+0 blitz for now)

## Weekly
- [ ] Import games: `python import_chess.py`
- [ ] Review 2-3 losses: Chess.com Game Review first, then fill in the game note
- [ ] Weekly review with Claude (see [Claude Prompts](Claude-Prompts.md))
- [ ] Update [Mistake Types](Tactics/Mistake-Types.md)

## Core habits
1. Before every move: [Pre-Move Checklist](Tactics/Pre-Move-Checklist.md)
2. Openings: [Repertoire](Openings/Repertoire.md) (one simple system, do not keep switching)
3. Endgames: king + rook mate, king + queen mate, king and pawn vs king
